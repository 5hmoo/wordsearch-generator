# Simple Wordsearch Generator
### [Open the Live Tool]([https://5hmoo.github.io/wordsearch-generator/index.html])

Generates a 20x20 letter grid wordsearch and solution from your input, which can then be printed or saved as an image.

## About this Project
This tool was built to provide a simple, clean way to generate wordsearches for print. Unlike many online generators, this tool runs entirely in your browser—meaning the text you enter is never sent to a server or stored. It is designed with accessibility and ease of use in mind.

## Features
- **High-Contrast Design:** Built with a 7:1 contrast ratio to ensure readability for all users.
- **Privacy First:** No data leaves your computer.
- **Export Options:** - **Print Ready:** Includes a clean layout for the puzzle and solution.
  - **Save as:** Export your puzzles as PNG or PDF files for easy sharing.

## How to use
1. Enter your title.
2. Enter your list of words, up to 20 letters per word.
3. Choose the difficulty level you want the puzzle to have.
4. Click **Generate New Puzzle** and **Show/Hide Solution** to see what the solution looks like.
5. You can regenerate the puzzle multiple times until you're happy with the layout of the solution.
6. Download the puzzle by clicking the **Download PNG** or **Download PDF** buttons.
7. Do not regenerate the puzzle between downloading the puzzle and the solution or the puzzle and solution won't match.
